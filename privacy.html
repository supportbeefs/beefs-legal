Beefs

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Beefs is a mobile app where you challenge other people to a beef - any competition, debate or duel - and settle it together. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why it is collected, who it is shared with, and how you can get it deleted.

The short version. We collect the account and profile details you type in, the profile picture you pick, and the beefs, messages, results and votes you create in the app. We use them to run the app and nothing else. We do not run ads, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data. Your profile and your public beefs are visible to other users of the app - everything else is private to you and the person you are beefing with.

1. Who we are

Beefs (Android package and iOS bundle identifier com.jovansreco.beefs) is an independent app published by Jovan Sreco. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, we are the data controller for the personal data described here.

You can reach us about anything in this policy, including access or deletion requests, at supportbeefs@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

All of it comes either from what you enter in the app or from what the app needs in order to work. We do not buy data about you from anyone.

Account

  • Email address and password. Used to create and sign in to your account. Your password is stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider - we cannot read it.
  • Account identifier and timestamps (when the account was created and last updated).

Profile

  • Display name and @handle - required, and public.
  • Location - optional free text you type yourself, such as a city or country. The app does not use GPS or any device location service.
  • Favourite categories - up to 20 categories you pick from a list.
  • Profile picture - required to finish setting up a profile. See Photos below.

Beefs and the content you create

  • Beef details - who you challenged, category, whether it is online or in person, the terms, rules, stake, deadlines, and whether the beef is public or private.
  • Chat messages you send inside a beef, and the lifecycle entries that go with it (created, accepted, rejected, cancelled, concluded).
  • Result offers you submit or respond to, and the notes attached to them.
  • Votes you cast on public beefs where the two participants disagree about the result.
  • Read state - which beefs you have opened, so unread indicators work.

Photos

When you tap to choose a profile picture, the app asks your device for permission to open your photo library. Only the single image you select is uploaded; the app does not scan, browse or upload anything else from your device, and it does not use your camera. Profile pictures are stored in a publicly readable bucket so they can be shown next to your name in the app.

Subscription (Beefs Pro)

If you buy a subscription, the purchase is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We receive only a subscription status (active, expired, cancelled) linked to an app-specific purchase identifier, through our subscription provider. Your payment card, billing address and full store account details never reach us.

Technical data

Our hosting provider records standard request logs when the app talks to our backend, including your IP address, timestamp, the endpoint called and error details. These logs exist for security, abuse prevention and debugging, and they are kept for a short period.

3. What other users can see

Beefs is a social app, so part of what you enter is deliberately visible to others. It is worth being precise about which part:

What Who can see it
Display name, @handle, location, favourite categories, profile picture Any signed-in user of the app
Email address, password Nobody but you - never shown to other users
A public beef: its terms, timeline, messages, offers and result Any signed-in user, plus anyone who opens a share link to that beef (a share link works only for public beefs)
A private beef and everything inside it Only the two participants
Votes on a public beef Participants and spectators see the totals; your individual vote is not shown to the participants
Subscription status Nobody but you

Choose "public" only for a beef you are happy for strangers to read. A concluded beef becomes a read-only transcript that both participants keep, which is why messages cannot be edited or deleted after the fact.

4. Why we use your data and our legal bases

If you are in the EU, UK or another region with similar law, these are the legal bases we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract - creating and maintaining your account, showing your profile, delivering challenges, chat, results and voting, and providing the features of a paid subscription. Without this data there is no app.
  • Legitimate interests - keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and spam, enforcing the rules of the beef lifecycle, diagnosing errors, and keeping a tamper-proof record of a concluded beef for both participants.
  • Consent - access to your photo library, which your device asks for and which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings (you will then not be able to change your profile picture).
  • Legal obligation - keeping records we are required to keep, for example in relation to purchases.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising networks, and there are no ads in the app.
  • We do not use analytics, attribution or tracking SDKs, and we do not build advertising profiles about you.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We do not collect your device location, contacts, calendar, microphone or health data.
  • We do not read your photo library beyond the one picture you select.

7. International transfers

Our providers may store and process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area or the UK, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another approved safeguard put in place by the provider.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and beefs - for as long as your account exists. Beefs are part of another person's history too, so they are not removed when they conclude.
  • After a deletion request - removed from our live systems within 30 days, and from encrypted backups within 90 days.
  • Technical logs - a short retention period at our hosting provider.
  • Purchase records - as long as required for accounting and tax purposes.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or out of date (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • delete your account and data (see the next section);
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • export your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw a consent you gave, such as photo library access.

Email supportbeefs@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will respond within 30 days. There is no charge. If you are in the EEA or the UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we will never discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

10. Deleting your account and data

To have your account deleted, email supportbeefs@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted:

  • your login credentials and email address;
  • your profile - display name, @handle, location, favourite categories - and your profile picture file;
  • your read state and your votes on other people's beefs.

What is kept, and in what form:

  • Beefs you took part in remain visible to the other participant as an anonymised record: the messages, offers and result stay, but they are no longer linked to your name, handle or picture. A beef is a shared history between two people, and erasing one side would destroy the other person's record of it.
  • Aggregate vote counts on public beefs remain, as they are numbers that no longer identify anyone.
  • Purchase and invoice records held by Apple, Google or our subscription provider are kept as long as the law requires.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings, otherwise it will keep renewing.

11. Children

Beefs is not intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it, or 16 if you are in the European Economic Area or the UK. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age; if you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We take the usual precautions and a few specific ones:

  • all traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted with TLS;
  • passwords are stored only as hashes, by our authentication provider;
  • database access is protected by row-level security, so a request can only read the rows that account is allowed to see;
  • every change to a beef goes through a server-side function that enforces the rules, rather than letting the app write to tables directly;
  • profile pictures can only be uploaded or replaced inside a folder tied to your own account.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

13. Links posted by users

Messages, rules, stakes and offer notes can contain links that other users typed. Tapping one opens it in your browser, outside Beefs. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Where a change is significant, we will also make it visible in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to use Beefs after a change means you accept the updated policy.

15. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: supportbeefs@gmail.com. We read every email.
